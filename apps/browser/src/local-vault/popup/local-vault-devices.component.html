Each device that shares the vault folder keeps its changes in a folder of its own there, named after the ID of the device. Retire a device that will not be used again, such as this extension before it was installed again: this device takes over what it kept, and its folder is deleted after 90 days. A device that is still used, but only to fill in passwords, may not have changed the vault for a long time either: tell the devices apart by their types and names.
